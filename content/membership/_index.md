Join our community of skiing and golf enthusiasts! Intershack membership provides access to year-round events, exclusive discounts, and a welcoming community that has been making memories at Jay Peak for over 57 years.

## Membership Benefits

### Events & Activities
- Annual Labor Day Golf Tournament
- Welcome Cocktail Party
- Holiday skating parties and social events
- St. Patrick's Day celebrations
- Valentine's Day dinners
- Jay Peak Tailgate parties

### Member Services
- **Fred's Energy Propane Program**: Exclusive discounts on home heating propane

### Community
- Connect with like-minded outdoor enthusiasts
- Multi-generational community of families and individuals
- Over 57 years of tradition and mountain memories
- Social network spanning Montreal and Vermont

## How to Join

**Annual Membership Fee**: $50 USD or CDN

1. **Complete the Membership Form**: [Click here to fill out the online membership form](/membership/form/)
2. **Submit Payment**: Mail your $50 check to:

   **Intershack**
   P.O. Box 493
   Montgomery Center, VT 05471

3. **Questions?**: Email [intershackgroup@gmail.com](mailto:intershackgroup@gmail.com) for assistance

### Membership Year
The Intershack membership year runs from May 1st to April 30th.
